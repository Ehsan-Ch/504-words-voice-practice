## Source sentences

1. I put a paper bookmark between the pages before making tea.
2. Please fill the kettle with fresh water.
3. I refill a bottle before returning to my book.

## Original story

Mina reads by the window after lunch. When she wants a cup of tea, she puts a
paper bookmark between the pages. She goes to the kitchen to fill the kettle.
While the water heats, she remembers to refill a bottle for her afternoon walk.
Soon her tea is ready. She returns to the window and opens her book at the marked
page. The bookmark has saved her place.
